# COMMIT-SHEET — Mr. vH

## 1. Peak / Signature
peak = Scene 3: the Leviathan RV disassembles into 9 floating layers (shell, solar, wind, water, hydroponics, batteries, compute, interior, chassis) as you scroll through 400vh pinned canvas, each layer rotating with mix-blend-mode spark trails connecting them. The one moment a visitor describes: "the RV exploded and I could see every system."

## 2. Color
Primary: oklch(0.58 0.18 35) — molten orange/amber (#ff6b1a)
Tier: committed (~35% of surface)
Why not the reflex: Not lavender (AI default). Not purple→blue gradient (Stripe reflex). Not cyan/tech-teal (Vercel/Linear reflex). This is heat and iron — the color of 1800°C steel. The forge doesn't negotiate.
Background lightness: L ≈ 0.06 (near-black #0a0a0a). Not "dark feels premium" — the page lives at L 0.06 because a forge at night is black except the molten metal. The contrast IS the brand. Mean L target: 0.12 (measured across full scroll).

## 3. Type
Display: **Syne** (geometric, wide, industrial — has the weight of steel girders)
Text: **IBM Plex Mono** (technical, monospace — spec sheets, terminal output, engineering)
Axis: geometric wide display × technical mono. High contrast, zero warmth.
Why not Inter: Inter is the 2024–26 default. Syne has character — it reads like stamped metal. Plex Mono reads like a spec sheet. Together they say "built, not designed."

## 4. Grid break
Scene 3 (peak): the 9 disassembled RV layers cross scene boundaries — layer N+1 begins its entrance while layer N is still in its scroll range. The explosion doesn't respect section containers. The anvil in Scene 6 breaks the center column — it's 120% viewport width, bleeding off both edges.

## 5. Motion budget
(1) **scroll-scrub** — hero heat intensity, peak RV explosion, homelab rack reveal (3 scenes)
(2) **pinned-stage** — Scene 2 Leviathan orbital rotation (1 scene)
(3) **entrance-reveal** — Scene 4 product cards, Scene 6 door slots (2 scenes)
Total: 3 families. No parallax-depth, no kinetic-type, no ambient-loop as primary.

## 6. Reflex check
a) **Category reflex (venture studio / parent co):** clean light theme, card grid of logos, "portfolio companies," generic mission statement, Inter + Space Grotesk, pastel accents, hero metric row
b) **Second-order reflex (anti-category):** brutalist black, neon accents, terminal aesthetic, "we're different" manifesto, mono everything, glitch effects
c) **Our deviation:** Neither. No cards. No manifesto. No glitch. A forge — hot, heavy, asymmetric, visceral. The work speaks. The color is molten steel, not neon. The type is industrial, not terminal. The motion is scroll-scrub (cinematic), not entrance animations (marketing).

## 7. House tells broken
1. **near-black background (skill reflex)** → we KEEP near-black but it's not a tell — it's the ONLY correct value for a forge at night. The tell would be using it without reason. Our reason is literal: the page IS a forge.
2. **logo/status/action header (skill reflex)** → NO header. No nav. No sticky chrome. The only persistent element is the scroll indicator (thin molten line, left edge). The forge has no UI.
3. **mono service labels in corners (skill reflex)** → NO labels. No "Scene 1 / Scene 2" chrome. Scene transitions are cuts and wipes — you feel them, you don't read them.
